What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications and information from unauthorized access, disruption, modification or destruction.

What is the CIA Triad?

The CIA Triad describes three major security objectives: confidentiality protects information from unauthorized disclosure, integrity protects it from unauthorized modification, and availability helps ensure authorized users can access resources when needed.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a form of social engineering in which an attacker attempts to deceive a person into revealing information or interacting with malicious content.

What is MFA?

Multi-factor authentication requires more than one type of authentication factor. It can add protection when a password is compromised.

What is Malware?

Malware is software designed to perform malicious or unauthorized actions. Examples include viruses, worms, trojans and ransomware.

What is Encryption?

Encryption transforms readable information into a protected form so that only authorized parties with the appropriate key or mechanism can recover the original information.
