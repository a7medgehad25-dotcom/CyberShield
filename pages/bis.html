📈 Microeconomics Notes

Click any topic to open your notes.

Scarcity & Opportunity Cost

Resources are limited while human wants are unlimited. Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative given up when making a choice.

Supply & Demand

Demand describes the quantity consumers are willing and able to buy at different prices. Supply describes the quantity producers are willing and able to sell.

Market Equilibrium

Market equilibrium occurs where quantity demanded and quantity supplied are equal.

Elasticity

Elasticity measures how responsive one variable is to a change in another variable, such as how quantity demanded responds to a change in price.

Fixed & Variable Costs

Fixed costs generally do not change with the level of output over a relevant range, while variable costs change as output changes.

🚀 Entrepreneurship Notes

Important concepts for future entrepreneurs.

Business Idea

A business idea identifies a problem, need or opportunity and proposes a product or service that can address it.

Business Model

A business model explains how an organization creates, delivers and captures value.

Market Research

Market research helps entrepreneurs understand customers, competitors, demand and market conditions.

MVP

A minimum viable product is an early version of a product designed to test important assumptions with users.

🧮 Business Mathematics Notes

Important calculations used in business.

Percentages

Percentages are commonly used to calculate changes, discounts, margins, growth rates and financial ratios.

Simple Interest

Simple interest is calculated using principal, interest rate and time.

Break-even Point

The break-even point is the level of sales at which total revenue equals total costs.

🧾 Accounting Notes

Essential accounting concepts.

Assets

Assets are economic resources controlled by a business, such as cash, inventory and equipment.

Liabilities

Liabilities are obligations that a business owes to other parties.

Equity

Equity represents the owner's residual interest after liabilities are deducted from assets.

🏢 Management Notes

Understand how organizations are managed.

Planning

Planning involves defining objectives and determining actions and resources needed to achieve them.

Organizing

Organizing involves arranging people, responsibilities and resources to achieve organizational goals.

Leading

Leading involves motivating and guiding people toward organizational objectives.

Controlling

Controlling involves monitoring performance and taking corrective action when necessary.
